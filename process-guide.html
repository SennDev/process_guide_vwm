Provisions Process

A simplified view of the claims presentation process for provisions, organized by meeting type, timing and requirements.

01
Wednesday 11:00 AM

Pre-Alignment

  • Deadline: Tuesday 12:00 PM.
  • Only Provisions Coordinators submit topics.
  • Official templates are mandatory.
  • Complete overview chart is required.
02
Tuesday 10:30 AM

COM Meeting

  • Only pre-aligned topics go to COM.
  • Buyer presents in COM following Tuesday.
  • Exception: Global Risk Management topics.
  • COM agenda closes Friday 12:00 PM.
03
Once a month

Sustainable Effects Release with Till

  • Only price increase topics.
  • Agenda closes Thursday previous to the meeting.
  • Friday 12:00 PM deadline to receive presentation.
